1 Introduction

Baryan Badminton Academy (“the Academy”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is committed to protecting the privacy and personal information of our members, parents, coaches, and website visitors.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your data.

By registering with the Academy or using our website, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2 Information We Collect

We collect the following types of personal information:

Information you provide directly

  • Full name, date of birth, and gender
  • Contact details (phone number, email address, physical address)
  • Parent/guardian information (for junior members)
  • Emergency contact details
  • Medical information relevant to safe participation (allergies, injuries, conditions)
  • Payment and membership details
  • Reviews, feedback, or messages you send us

Information collected automatically

  • Website usage data (pages visited, time spent, browser type)
  • IP address and device information
  • Cookies and similar technologies (see section 6)

3 How We Use Your Information

We use personal information only for legitimate Academy purposes, including:

  • Managing memberships, registrations, and training schedules
  • Communicating with members and parents about sessions, events, and important updates
  • Ensuring player safety and providing appropriate coaching
  • Processing payments and maintaining financial records
  • Improving our programmes, website, and services
  • Responding to enquiries and providing customer support
  • Complying with legal obligations
  • Promoting Academy activities (with consent for photos/videos – see our Terms)

4 Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

We may share information only in the following limited situations:

  • With coaches and staff who need the information to perform their duties
  • With service providers who help us operate (e.g. payment processors, website hosting) under strict confidentiality
  • When required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of the Academy or its members
  • With your explicit consent

5 Data Storage & Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

  • Data is stored securely using trusted platforms (including Firebase for reviews and forms).
  • Access to personal data is limited to authorised Academy personnel only.
  • We regularly review our security practices.

While we strive to protect your data, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. You share information at your own risk.

6 Cookies & Website Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve your experience, analyse traffic, and remember preferences.

  • Essential cookies help the website function properly.
  • Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors use the site.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some website features.

7 Children’s Privacy

Many of our members are children and young people. We take extra care with their personal information.

  • Registration of players under 18 must be completed by a parent or legal guardian.
  • We only collect information necessary for membership, safety, and coaching.
  • Photos and videos of minors are used only with parental consent (as outlined in our Terms & Conditions).
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

8 Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access – Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction – Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion – Request deletion of your data (subject to legal or operational requirements)
  • Objection – Object to certain uses of your data
  • Withdraw consent – Where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in section 11. We will respond within a reasonable time.

9 Data Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by law.

  • Membership records are retained while you are an active member and for a reasonable period afterwards.
  • Financial records are kept as required by Ugandan law.
  • When data is no longer needed, it is securely deleted or anonymised.

10 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements.

The latest version will always be available on this page, with the “Last updated” date clearly shown. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11 Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us:

  • Email: baryanmintonclub@gmail.com
  • WhatsApp: +256 755 805 092

We are committed to resolving any privacy-related issues promptly and fairly.
