TRAINING LEVELS

FIND YOUR SESSION.

01

Beginner

Perfect for players who are new to badminton or still learning the fundamentals.

  • Grip
  • Serving
  • Basic strokes
  • Footwork
  • Court positioning
02

Intermediate

For players who understand the fundamentals and want to improve consistency and match performance.

  • Rally development
  • Advanced footwork
  • Tactical play
  • Match awareness
  • Consistency
03

Advanced

Performance-focused training for experienced players preparing for competitive badminton.

  • Advanced tactics
  • Match preparation
  • Fitness
  • Strategy
  • Competition preparation
04

Junior

Age-appropriate training that builds confidence, coordination and badminton fundamentals.

  • Coordination
  • Basic movement
  • Racket skills
  • Fun games
  • Sportsmanship
