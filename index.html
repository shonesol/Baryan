ABOUT THE ACADEMY

MORE THAN A BADMINTON ACADEMY.

Baryan Badminton Academy is committed to developing badminton players through structured coaching, skills development, fitness, discipline and teamwork.

Our goal is to create an environment where young athletes and badminton players can learn the fundamentals, improve their performance and develop the character required both on and off the court.

Discover our programs →

OUR PURPOSE

BUILDING BETTER PLAYERS.

Baryan Badminton Academy combines badminton development with discipline, teamwork and personal growth.

M

Our Mission

To develop badminton players through quality coaching, consistent training, discipline, teamwork and opportunities to compete.

V

Our Vision

To become a respected badminton development academy that creates opportunities for athletes to reach their full potential.

WHY JOIN US
