4 Code of Conduct

All members, parents, and visitors are expected to maintain a high standard of behaviour that reflects the values of the Academy: discipline, respect, teamwork, and sportsmanship.

  • Treat coaches, staff, fellow players, and officials with respect at all times.
  • Follow all instructions given by coaches and Academy staff.
  • No bullying, harassment, discrimination, or abusive language will be tolerated.
  • Do not engage in any behaviour that endangers the safety of others.
  • Respect Academy property and training facilities.

Serious or repeated breaches of the Code of Conduct may result in temporary suspension or permanent termination of membership without refund.
