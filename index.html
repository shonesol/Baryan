Our Mission & Vision

The heart and soul of every adventure we create at Tujje Safaris

Our Mission

To deliver authentic, safe and unforgettable safari experiences that connect travellers with the wild beauty of Uganda and East Africa, while supporting local communities and protecting wildlife for future generations.

  • Create personalized, high-quality safari journeys
  • Support conservation and community projects
  • Provide expert local guides and seamless service
  • Promote sustainable and responsible tourism
  • Make Africa accessible to every kind of traveller

Our Vision

To become the most trusted and preferred safari company in East Africa — known for exceptional experiences, genuine care, and a lasting positive impact on people, wildlife and the environment.

  • Be the first choice for gorilla trekking & wildlife safaris
  • Set the standard for sustainable safari tourism
  • Inspire a new generation of responsible travellers
  • Grow alongside the communities we work with
  • Leave every guest with lifelong memories of Africa

Meet Our Expert Guides

Passionate local experts who turn every safari into a lifetime memory. Their knowledge, energy and love for Uganda will make your journey unforgettable.

Huzo

Lead Safari Guide & Gorilla Specialist

With over 12 years of experience in Bwindi and Mgahinga, Huzo has led more than 800 successful gorilla treks. Known for his calm energy, sharp wildlife spotting skills and incredible storytelling around the campfire.

Kirunda

Wildlife & Birding Expert

Kirunda is a true son of Queen Elizabeth and Murchison Falls National Parks. A certified bird guide with over 400 species under his belt, he brings the savannah to life with infectious enthusiasm and deep ecological knowledge.

Andrew

Adventure & Cultural Guide

From white-water rafting on the Nile to cultural immersions with the Batwa and Banyankole, Andrew crafts high-energy, heartfelt experiences. Guests love his big smile, safety-first approach and talent for creating magical group moments.
