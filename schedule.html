TRAINING LEVELS

CHOOSE YOUR LEVEL.

01

Beginner

Perfect for players who are new to badminton or want to build a strong foundation.

  • Grip and racket control
  • Basic footwork
  • Serving
  • Basic strokes
02

Intermediate

For players who already understand badminton fundamentals and want to improve their performance.

  • Advanced footwork
  • Rally consistency
  • Tactical play
  • Match preparation
03

Advanced

Performance-focused training for experienced players preparing for competitive badminton.

  • Match strategy
  • High-intensity drills
  • Tactical development
  • Competition preparation
04

Junior

Age-appropriate training that helps young players develop coordination, confidence and badminton skills.

  • Fun skill development
  • Coordination
  • Movement
  • Sportsmanship

Arrive Early

02

Bring Equipment

Bring your racket, appropriate sports clothing, shoes and water.

03

Respect Others

Respect coaches, players, officials, facilities and other court users.

04

Stay Disciplined

Follow academy instructions and maintain good sportsmanship.
